=== RISK SCORING (LIKELIHOOD x IMPACT) ===

Score the extracted risk on two 5-point scales (NIST AI RMF / ISO 31000 style,
informed by the FAIR model). Base scores ONLY on evidence in the article.
Provide exactly ONE sentence of reasoning for each score.

LIKELIHOOD — probability the risk occurs or recurs. Consider FAIR Loss Event
Frequency factors: how often threat events like this occur, and how susceptible
the affected systems/population are.
1 = Rare — theoretical/speculative; no occurrence described and strong barriers exist
2 = Unlikely — could occur but described as an edge case or well mitigated
3 = Possible — plausible under normal conditions; near-misses or pilot findings reported
4 = Likely — incident already occurred once, or conditions make recurrence probable
5 = Almost Certain — recurring/widespread incidents, or a systemic flaw affecting all users

IMPACT — severity of consequences if the risk occurs. Consider FAIR Loss
Magnitude: the scale of primary and secondary losses.
1 = Negligible — minimal harm; easily reversible; no material, legal, or safety effect
2 = Minor — limited harm to few individuals or one organization; recoverable
3 = Moderate — meaningful harm: financial loss, privacy violation, or reputational damage
4 = Major — serious harm at scale: large breach, discrimination affecting many, major outage
5 = Severe — catastrophic: loss of life, critical-infrastructure failure, societal-scale harm

LOSS CATEGORIES — list ALL that apply from FAIR's six forms of loss, based on
the harms described or clearly implied in the article:
"Productivity", "Response", "Replacement", "Fines & Judgments",
"Competitive Advantage", "Reputation"

Rules:
- likelihood and impact must be integers 1-5, or null if the article gives
  no basis for a score. Use null rather than guessing.
- likelihood_reasoning / impact_reasoning: one sentence each, citing article evidence.
- Do NOT compute a combined severity number; it is derived downstream as
  likelihood x impact.
